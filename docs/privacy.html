Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • Pact has no servers of its own. Your data is stored on your iPhone and in Apple's iCloud, in your account and, for what you share as a couple, in the iCloud account of the partner who created the couple.
  • We (the developer) cannot see your habits, check-ins, photos, notes or Apple Health data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and we never sell or share your data.
  • Your partner sees what you do in the app together. Your daily progress photos are private unless you choose to share them.
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time (Profile → Delete my data).

1. Who we are

Pact (“the app”) is made by Tomás Beja, an independent developer based in Portugal (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the limited data described in section 6. Contact: pact.couples.app@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Pact app for iPhone, its widgets, Live Activity and Apple Watch app.

2. Where your data is stored

Pact uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and does not run its own servers:

  • The couple space. When someone starts a couple, Pact creates a private space in their iCloud account and shares it with their partner through iCloud sharing. Everything the two of you do together is stored there. Only the two members of the couple can access it.
  • Your own iCloud. Your daily progress photos and an app identifier (used to recognise you after reinstalling) are stored in your own private iCloud.
  • Your devices. A copy of your data is kept on your iPhone so the app works offline. Widgets, the Live Activity and the Apple Watch app receive a small summary from your iPhone (names, today's habits and their status, streak).
  • iCloud's public database, only for an invite code while it is open, and for the optional Android waitlist (section 6).

iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You need an iCloud account to use Pact.

4. What your partner can see

Pact is a shared app: your partner can see what you add to the couple space, including your name and avatar, your habits, check-ins and their photos, reactions, comments, notes, bets, your subscription status, and your streak and progress. Your daily progress photos are not shared unless you turn on “Share progress pics with partner” in Profile; turning it off again removes the shared copies.

Only take proof photos you are comfortable sharing, and don't photograph other people without their consent.

5. Apple Health

If you turn on “Use Apple Health as proof” and allow access, Pact reads (never writes) today's step count, exercise minutes, water and mindful minutes on your iPhone, to show your progress and check off matching habits. The readings stay on your iPhone. When a habit is checked off from Apple Health, a one-line summary is saved with that check-in so your partner sees it (for example “Apple Health · 8,412 steps”).

Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone other than your partner as described above. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings, and turn the feature off in Profile.

6. What we (the developer) can access

We cannot access your private iCloud or your couple space. The only personal data we can access is:

  • Open invite codes. While an invite is open, iCloud's public database holds the 6-letter code, the inviter's first name, the iCloud sharing link and the date it was made, so the partner can join with the code. It is deleted when the partner joins, when a new code is made, or when the creator leaves. Anyone who has the code can see the inviter's first name.
  • The Android waitlist (optional). If you tell us your partner isn't on iPhone and choose to join the waitlist, we store the email address you enter so we can tell you when an Android version exists. We use it for nothing else. To have it deleted, email us.
  • Emails you send us for support.
  • Information from Apple. Apple gives us sales and subscription reports, and crash reports and app analytics only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings. These do not identify you to us by name. Pact sends Apple a random identifier with each purchase, which is not linked to your name or Apple ID on our side.

7. Device permissions and on-device features

  • Camera: to take live proof photos and progress photos. Photos are taken in the app; Pact doesn't read your photo library.
  • Photo library (add only): only when you ask to save your timelapse video.
  • Notifications: reminders, your partner's check-ins, nudges and your weekly recap. You choose which ones in the app, and can turn them off in iOS Settings. Notifications and the Live Activity can show names and habits on your Lock Screen.
  • On-device suggestions: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Pact uses Apple's on-device language model to turn what you type (like “learn to speak Chinese”) into a short habit name and ideas. This runs on your iPhone; the text is not sent to us.

8. Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

9. No ads, no tracking, no selling

Pact contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell, rent or share personal data.

10. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Delete my data (Profile) removes you from the couple, deletes your progress photos from your iPhone and your iCloud, and clears the app's data on your iPhone.
  • If you created the couple, leaving or deleting your data deletes the whole couple space for both of you, including your partner's check-ins and photos in it.
  • If you joined a couple, leaving removes your access and your shared progress photo copies. Your past check-ins stay in the couple space, which lives in your partner's iCloud, until they leave or delete their data. You can ask your partner to do so.
  • Otherwise, data is kept until you delete it. You can also manage or delete Pact's iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Invite codes are deleted as described in section 6. Waitlist emails are kept until an Android version is announced or you ask us to delete them.

11. Legal bases (EU and UK users)

We process personal data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), and on the basis of your consent for Apple Health access, the camera, notifications and the Android waitlist. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do directly in the app and in iCloud, because the data is in your own account. For anything else, email pact.couples.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, cnpd.pt), or the authority where you live or work.

13. Children

Pact is not meant for children. You must be at least 16 years old (or older, where local law requires) to use it. We do not knowingly process data of children under 16; if you believe a child has used Pact, contact us.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
