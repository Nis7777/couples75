Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The short version

  • Pact has no servers of its own. Your data lives on your iPhone and your partner's. What you share as a couple travels between your two phones through Pact's own iCloud database at Apple, encrypted on your phones with a key only the two of you have. It doesn't use your personal iCloud storage (only the optional backup of your own progress photos does).
  • We (the developer) cannot see your habits, check-ins, photos, notes or Apple Health data. Apple can't read what passes between your phones either: it only stores scrambled data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and we never sell or share your data.
  • Your partner sees what you do in the app together. Your daily progress photos are private unless you choose to share them.
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time (Profile → Delete my data).

1. Who we are

Pact (“the app”) is made by Tomás Beja, an independent developer based in Portugal (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the limited data described in section 6. Contact: pact.couples.app@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Pact app for iPhone, its widgets and Live Activity.

2. Where your data is stored

Pact uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and does not run its own servers:

  • Your iPhone. Everything you see in the app is kept on your iPhone, so it works offline. Widgets and the Live Activity receive a small summary from your iPhone (names, today's habits and their status, streak).
  • Between the two of you. What you do together (habits, check-ins and their photos, reactions, notes) is sent to your partner's iPhone through Pact's own iCloud database at Apple. Before it leaves your iPhone it is encrypted (AES-256) with a key that is created on the phone of the partner who starts the couple and handed to the other partner through the invite code. Without that key the stored data is unreadable, to Apple and to us. It does not count against your personal iCloud storage.
  • Photos only pass through. A proof photo (or a progress photo you chose to share) is removed from the iCloud database once your partner's iPhone has received it, and after 30 days at the latest. Each of your iPhones keeps its own copy.
  • Your own iCloud. Your daily progress photos are backed up to your own private iCloud when there is space, protected by Apple like the rest of your iCloud (end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection). Your app identifier and the couple's key are kept in your iCloud key-value storage, so you can get your pact back after reinstalling the app.
  • iCloud's public database, only for an invite code while it is open, and for the optional waitlist for partners without an iPhone (section 6).

iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You need an iCloud account to use Pact.

4. What your partner can see

Pact is a shared app: your partner can see what you share in the app, including your name and avatar, your habits, check-ins and their photos, reactions, comments, notes, bets, your subscription status, and your streak and progress. Your daily progress photos are not shared unless you turn on “Share progress pics with partner” in Profile; turning it off again removes the shared copies from your partner's iPhone.

Only take proof photos you are comfortable sharing, and don't photograph other people without their consent.

5. Apple Health

If you turn on “Use Apple Health as proof” and allow access, Pact reads (never writes) today's step count, exercise minutes, water and mindful minutes on your iPhone, to show your progress and check off matching habits. The readings stay on your iPhone: they are never sent to your partner, to iCloud or to us. When a habit is checked off this way, your partner only sees that it was “Checked off automatically”, never your numbers.

Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never stored in iCloud and never shared with anyone, including your partner. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings, and turn the feature off in Profile.

6. What we (the developer) can access

We cannot access your private iCloud, and what passes between your phones (and your name in an open invite) is encrypted with keys we never have. The only personal data we can access is:

  • Open invite codes. While an invite is open, iCloud's public database holds the date it was made, and the inviter's first name and the couple's key, both locked with the 6-letter code, so the partner can join with the code. We can't read the name or the key. The record is named by a one-way hash of the code, not the code itself, and invite records can't be listed, so they can only be found by someone who already has the code. It is deleted when the partner joins, when a new code is made, or when the creator leaves. Only someone who has the code can see the inviter's first name. The invite link you share contains the code and your first name after a “#”, a part of a link that browsers never send to the web server, so our website host never receives them.
  • The waitlist (optional). If you tell us your partner doesn't have an iPhone and choose to join the waitlist, we store the email address you enter so we can tell you when Pact works for them. We use it for nothing else. To have it deleted, email us.
  • Emails you send us for support.
  • Information from Apple. Apple gives us sales and subscription reports, and crash reports and app analytics only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings. These do not identify you to us by name. Pact sends Apple a random identifier with each purchase, which is not linked to your name or Apple ID on our side.

7. Device permissions and on-device features

  • Camera: to take proof photos and progress photos. Photos are taken in the app; Pact doesn't read your photo library.
  • Photo library (add only): only when you ask to save your timelapse video or a milestone card. Pact never reads your photo library. Milestone cards contain no proof photo unless you turn on “Include a proof photo”.
  • Notifications: reminders, your partner's check-ins, nudges and your weekly recap. You choose which ones in the app, and can turn them off in iOS Settings. Notifications and the Live Activity can show names and habits on your Lock Screen.
  • On-device suggestions: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Pact uses Apple's on-device language model to turn what you type (like “learn to speak Chinese”) into a short habit name and ideas. This runs on your iPhone; the text is not sent to us.

8. Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

9. No ads, no tracking, no selling

Pact contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell, rent or share personal data.

10. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Delete my data (Profile) removes you from the couple, deletes everything you wrote from the iCloud database, deletes your progress photos from your iPhone and your iCloud, and clears the app's data on your iPhone. A small encrypted note that you left stays, so your partner's app knows.
  • Leaving the couple does the same for your data. Your partner's data is deleted when they leave or delete their data too; the app asks them to once you've left.
  • Photos are removed from the iCloud database once delivered, and after 30 days at the latest. Otherwise data is kept until you delete it. You can also manage Pact's data in your own iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Invite codes are deleted as described in section 6. Waitlist emails are kept until we've told you Pact works for your partner, or until you ask us to delete them.

11. Legal bases (EU and UK users)

We process personal data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), and on the basis of your consent for Apple Health access, the camera, notifications and the waitlist. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do directly in the app and in iCloud, because the data is in your own account. For anything else, email pact.couples.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, cnpd.pt), or the authority where you live or work.

13. Children

Pact is not meant for children. You must be at least 13 years old (or older, where local law requires) to use it. We do not knowingly process data of children under 13; if you believe a child has used Pact, contact us.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
