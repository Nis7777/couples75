Getting started

How do my partner and I connect?

One of you starts Pact, sets up their habits and subscribes. The app then gives you an invite link and a 6-letter code. Your partner installs Pact, taps My partner invited me and enters the code (or opens the link). One subscription covers you both: your partner joins free.

My partner started the app too. Will we pay twice?

Only the person who starts the couple subscribes. If your partner started setting up by mistake, they should go back to the first screen, tap My partner invited me (or Already invited? Join with your partner's code on the subscription screen) and enter your code.

When does Day 1 start?

The day after your partner joins. Each day ends at midnight in each person's own time zone, so it works for long-distance couples too.

iCloud

Why do I need iCloud?

Pact stores and shares your data through Apple's iCloud instead of its own servers. Turn on iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud. If your iCloud storage is full, photo check-ins can't upload; free up space or use an honour tap for that day.

My partner's check-in hasn't arrived.

Check that both phones are online and signed in to iCloud, then pull down on Today to refresh. Check-ins have to reach iCloud within 6 hours of being taken to count.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

In iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Pact. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phones and Pact asks me to subscribe.

Tap Restore on the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

Apple Health

Turn on Use Apple Health as proof in Profile and allow access. Steps, exercise minutes, water and mindful minutes then check off matching habits by themselves. Pact only reads from Health; it never writes to it.

Your data

To delete your data, go to Profile → Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what that removes, including what happens to the couple's shared data.
